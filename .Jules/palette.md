## 2025-05-14 - Accessible Single-Page Navigation
**Learning:** In a single-page application with a sticky header and smooth scrolling, standard anchor links often fail to update the accessibility tree's focus and can result in unreliable "active" state tracking in the navigation. The optimal `IntersectionObserver` `rootMargin` for a ~80px sticky header is `'-80px 0px -40% 0px'` to ensure even short sections trigger correctly. Programmatic focus management (using `tabindex="-1"` and `.focus({ preventScroll: true })`) is essential for screen reader users, but requires a `setTimeout` (approx. 600ms) to sync with smooth scroll animations.
**Action:** Always pair `IntersectionObserver` with `aria-current="page"` and programmatic focus shifts on navigation clicks. Use `[tabindex="-1"]:focus { outline: none; }` to prevent unwanted visual rings on section containers while maintaining accessibility.
