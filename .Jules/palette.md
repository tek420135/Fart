## 2025-05-14 - Accessible Scroll-Spy Navigation
**Learning:** Implementing scroll-spy requires more than just visual cues. To be truly accessible, smooth scrolling must be paired with programmatic focus management (shifting focus to the target section with `tabindex="-1"`) and URL hash synchronization (using `history.pushState` to avoid browser jumps). Additionally, in single-page layouts where not every section has a corresponding nav link, the IntersectionObserver logic needs a fallback to highlight the "parent" or "nearest preceding" nav item to maintain a clear sense of place for the user.
**Action:** Always pair smooth scroll with focus shifts and history updates. Use the `data-text` + `::after` CSS pattern to prevent layout shifts when toggling bold states on active navigation links.
