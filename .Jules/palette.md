## 2025-05-15 - Enhancing single-page orientation and keyboard visibility
**Learning:** In single-page applications with transparent or glass-morphism UI, standard browser focus rings can be invisible or clash with the design. Providing a high-contrast, theme-consistent `:focus-visible` ring (e.g., using `--mint`) is essential for both accessibility and visual harmony. Furthermore, programmatically managing `aria-current` via `IntersectionObserver` is only half the UX solution; it must be paired with clear visual styles (like color and font-weight shifts) to provide feedback to sighted users.
**Action:** Always verify that programmatic accessibility states (like `aria-current`) have corresponding visual representations and ensure focus indicators are tested against the specific background textures of the app.
